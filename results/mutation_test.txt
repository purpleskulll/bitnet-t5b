killed" (paper/arxiv.md, paper/bitnet-baremetal-t5b.tex) refers to the CURRENT
block. A reader going top-down and stopping at the first "killed 12
survived 0" gets the superseded count.

The superseded run is kept rather than overwritten, because this project
appends. It costs nothing to keep: all fourteen of its rows are reproduced
character-for-character in the current run, same failure counts.
