NOTE ADDED 2026-09-13. This is the FIVE-round run of the build that carries the
per-phase probes inside tinyBLAS_I2S_AVX, not the nine-round run the paper's
section 7.4 quotes. Its ratio is 1.379 against that run's 1.257, and the
difference is the probes themselves: they sit on the i2_s arm only and fire per
tile, inflating the arm they measure by about 10 percent. That is why the
shipped measurement puts them behind -DBITNET_I2S_PHASES and why the numbers in
the paper come from the build without them (results/insitu_symmetric.txt).

This file overwrote that one in commit fc0d2a6 and the nine-round data was
recovered from blob 3156e85 to restore it. Kept here under its own name so the
two runs cannot be confused again.
